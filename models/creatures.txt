# Voxel creatures, one per Rogue display character.
#
#   = <char> <name>
#   <front 8> <side 8>      one line per voxel layer, top layer first
#
# front is the creature seen from the camera side: each character is a
# material key from materials.txt, "." is empty. side is its silhouette
# seen from its right: column 0 is the back, column 7 the front, "#" solid.
# A voxel exists where the front has a material AND the side is solid on
# that layer, so two flat drawings carve one 3D shape. Layers left off the
# bottom do not exist; blank layers at the bottom make a creature hover.
# At most 14 layers.
#
# A third group of 8 is the back view, as seen from behind, for the camera
# that turns: it colours the back half of the layer. Without it the back
# half takes the layer's commonest material.
