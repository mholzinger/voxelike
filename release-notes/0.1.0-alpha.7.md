**Weapon-specific voxel deaths.** The killing blow now changes how the enemy falls. A mace pummels its target into a pool, a sword splits it down the middle, and a two-handed sword separates it into horizontal sections. Bow shots show the fatal arrow in flight and drive the target back. Hand-thrown arrows, bow strikes, daggers, darts, shuriken, and spears each have their own finish.

- **The hero can die in different ways too.** Enemy strikes and fatal bolts have distinct animations and effects. A crushing blow drives the hero into the floor, with one last voxel bubble at the surface before the camera moves on.
- **Bones show in cross-section.** The hero and several humanoid enemies, including orcs, hobgoblins, and trolls, reveal internal bones where a cut exposes them. Armor and clothing remain visible around the cut.
- **Try the animations in the Bestiary.** Open **Options → Bestiary**. **Player** is available from the start with your current gear. Defeated creatures unlock as you play. Press **A** to preview an attack, **C** for the next death, or **Start** to replay the selected death.
- **Edit voxel models in the browser.** Run `make edit` from the source checkout to paint voxels in 3D or edit individual slices. The editor saves back to the model files used by the ROM build.
- **Transparent gaps stay open.** The model baker and 3D renderers preserve empty voxels, including the gaps around the stock hero’s arms and between his boots.
