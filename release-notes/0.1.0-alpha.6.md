**A new renderer.** Rooms, passages and dark rooms are now drawn as textured polygons, with both of the 32X's processors painting half the picture each. The old ray renderer still draws the arrival, the bestiary and the title.

- **Faster.** Most views now hold 30 frames a second, and small ones 60, where a room used to run at about 20. A level that is half passages draws about three and a half times faster than it did.
- **Passages show all you have found.** A corridor is drawn as far as you have explored it, nine tiles ahead the way you are looking, and not only the two tiles round you.
- **Walls, doors and stairs have their shapes.** Walls are half a tile thick and stand against their room, with a ledge outside. A doorway is the mouth of a tunnel. Stairs sit down in the floor.
- **A map in the corner.** It shows where you have been, and the monsters you can see as red dots.
- **The hero turns.** Reverse direction in a tight spot and he is seen side on for a moment on the way round.
- **A new opening.** The Sega logo flies in out of the dark, built from its own letters, and past you. Start skips it.

### Known problems
- Levels that are mostly maze are the slowest thing in the game, and can drop to 20 frames a second.
- A message can cover the corner map while it is on screen.

### If you build it yourself
`make` now builds the polygon renderer. `make POLY=` gives everything back to the rays, and `make POLY=1` draws the polygons in flat colours. The README has the rest.
